# 🚀 AI Features Quick Start Guide

## Welcome to KSYK Maps AI! 🤖

Your campus just got a whole lot smarter with AI-powered features.

## 🎯 Quick Access

### Option 1: Header Button
Look for the **purple "✨ AI Assistant"** button in the top navigation bar (it's animated!)

### Option 2: Direct URL
Navigate to: **`/ai-assistant`**

## 🛠️ Available Features

### 1. 💬 AI Chat Assistant
**Two Modes:**
- **Study Buddy** 📚: Helps with homework and learning
- **Campus Assistant** 🗺️: Answers campus navigation questions

**How to use:**
1. Click "Study Buddy" or "Campus Assistant"
2. Type your question
3. Get instant AI responses with streaming!

**Example questions:**
- "Help me understand calculus derivatives"
- "Where is the science lab?"
- "How do I get to the cafeteria?"

---

### 2. 🗺️ AI Room Finder
Find any room on campus using natural language!

**How to use:**
1. Type your query (e.g., "Where is the math classroom?")
2. Click search
3. Get room number, building, floor, and confidence score

**Example queries:**
- "Find room A205"
- "Where is the gym?"
- "Math classroom location"

---

### 3. 📚 Homework Helper
Get help with any subject, with image support!

**How to use:**
1. Enter subject (Math, Physics, etc.)
2. Type your question
3. Optionally upload an image of the problem
4. Get educational explanations

**Supported:**
- All subjects
- Image uploads (diagrams, equations, etc.)
- Step-by-step explanations

---

### 4. 📅 Schedule Optimizer
Optimize your study schedule with AI!

**How to use:**
1. Paste your schedule as JSON
2. Click "Optimize Schedule"
3. Get personalized suggestions

**Example JSON:**
```json
{
  "classes": [
    {"name": "Math", "time": "9:00", "room": "A101"},
    {"name": "Physics", "time": "11:00", "room": "B205"}
  ]
}
```

---

### 5. 🖼️ Image Analysis
Analyze campus images with AI!

**How to use:**
1. Upload a campus image
2. Click "Analyze Image"
3. Get detailed description and identification

**Use cases:**
- Identify buildings
- Find facilities
- Understand campus layout

---

### 6. ♿ Accessibility Routes
Find wheelchair-friendly paths!

**How to use:**
1. Enter starting location
2. Enter destination
3. Select accessibility needs (Wheelchair, Elevator, etc.)
4. Get step-by-step accessible directions

**Features:**
- Elevator access
- Ramp locations
- Wide corridors
- Accessibility scoring

---

### 7. 💡 Event Suggestions
Get personalized campus event ideas!

**How to use:**
1. Enter your interests (comma-separated)
2. Click "Get Event Suggestions"
3. Discover events tailored to you

**Example interests:**
- sports, music, coding
- art, science, drama
- gaming, robotics, debate

---

### 8. 🔍 Smart Search
AI-powered search that understands you!

**How to use:**
1. Type your search query
2. AI understands typos and context
3. Get ranked, relevant results

**Features:**
- Typo correction
- Synonym understanding
- Context-aware
- Relevance scoring

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Getting Better Results
1. **Be Specific**: "Help me with quadratic equations" vs "Help with math"
2. **Use Context**: Mention what you've tried or what you know
3. **Ask Follow-ups**: AI remembers conversation history
4. **Upload Images**: For visual problems, upload a photo

### Chat Tips
- Start with "Study Buddy" for homework
- Use "Campus Assistant" for navigation
- Ask follow-up questions for clarification
- Be conversational - AI understands natural language

### Study Planner Tips
- List all your subjects
- Be specific about goals
- Mention time constraints
- Review and adjust the plan

### Language Learning Tips
- Practice daily conversations
- Use grammar checker regularly
- Start with beginner level
- Progress gradually

### Virtual Tour Tips
- Try different tour types
- Ask the guide questions
- Take your time at each stop
- Explore hidden gems tour for fun!
