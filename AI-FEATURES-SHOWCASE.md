## 💡 Pro Tips

### Getting Best Results
1. **Be Specific**: "Help with quadratic equations" > "Help with math"
2. **Use Context**: Mention what you've tried
3. **Ask Follow-ups**: AI remembers conversation
4. **Upload Images**: For visual problems

### Chat Tips
- Start with Study Buddy for homework
- Use Campus Assistant for navigation
- Be conversational
- Ask clarifying questions

### Study Planner Tips
- List all subjects
- Be specific about goals
- Mention time constraints
- Review and adjust

### Language Learning Tips
- Practice daily
- Use grammar checker
- Start at your level
- Progress gradually

### Virtual Tour Tips
- Try different tour types
- Ask guide questions
- Take your time
- Explore hidden gems!
